Tokens are what you spend to generate on Soundverse. Every tool shows its price before you run it.

## Where to see your balance

Click your name at the bottom of the left sidebar: the menu shows how many tokens you have left. **Manage your plan** shows more detail.

## How your balance works

- **Plan tokens** reset every month to your plan's allowance. Unused plan tokens don't roll over.
- **Top-up tokens** are kept separately and never expire. Your plan tokens are used first.
- **Free plan**: you get 1,000 one-time trial tokens, enough for about 3 full songs with Soundverse v6.

## What things cost

Prices are per run, by tool and model version, not by how long the result is:

| Tool | Tokens |
| --- | --- |
| Song or instrumental, Soundverse v6 | 300 |
| Song or instrumental, Soundverse v7 or v8 | 400 |
| Song or instrumental, Soundverse v9 | 500 |
| Extend an instrumental | 200 |
| Extend a song, Soundverse v6 | 300 |
| Extend a song, Soundverse v7 or v8 | 400 |
| Stem separation, 2 stems | 200 |
| Stem separation, 6 stems | 300 |
| Stem separation, 14+ stems with MIDI | 800 |
| AI Remix | 800 |
| Rewrite one section of a song | 200 |

Video, speech and sound-effect tools are priced by length. ElevenMusic v2.5 is priced by the length of the track. Each tool's page on [soundverse.ai/features](https://www.soundverse.ai/features) lists its current price.

## Agent One

When Agent One runs a tool for you, you pay the tool's price plus Agent One's own steps: about 7 tokens a step in Lite mode, 54 in Standard and 128 in Pro.

## Running out

On a paid plan, buy a top-up from **Manage your plan** → **Upgrades & top-ups**, or upgrade your plan. See [Plans and Pricing](/account/plans-and-pricing).
