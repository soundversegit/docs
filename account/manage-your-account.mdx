## Password 
If you forgot your password, go to the [sign-in page](https://www.soundverse.ai/login), click **Forgot password** and follow the steps. We email a verification code to the address you signed up with, and then you choose a new password. If you sign in with Google, there's no Soundverse password to reset: use your Google account.

If you believe your account has been compromised, reset your password immediately and contact support@soundverse.ai.

## Subscription 
To compare plans and prices, see https://www.soundverse.ai/pricing. To change or cancel your plan, click your name at the bottom of the left sidebar and choose **Manage your plan**.


## Tokens 
Click your name at the bottom of the left sidebar: the menu shows how many tokens you have left. **Manage your plan** shows your balance in more detail, and lets you buy a top-up on a paid plan.

Your plan's tokens reset every month. Unused plan tokens don't roll over: any plan tokens left at the end of the month expire, and your balance is set back to your plan's monthly allowance when your plan renews. Tokens you buy as a top-up are kept separately and don't expire.
