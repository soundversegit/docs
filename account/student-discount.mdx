Verified students aged 13 and up get **30% off** the Creator, Pro and Max plans.

## How to verify

1. Go to [soundverse.ai/education](https://www.soundverse.ai/education) and sign in.
2. Enter your age and your school email address.
3. We email a 6-digit code to your school address. Enter it within 10 minutes.
4. Choose a plan. The discount is applied automatically at checkout.

## Good to know

- Soundverse student accounts are for ages 13 and up.
- A verification lasts a year. After that, confirm your school email again to keep the discount.
- For a plan for your whole school or institution, write to sales@soundverse.ai.
