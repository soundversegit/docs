## Mobile apps

Soundverse is on iPhone and Android. Sign in with the same account you use on the web.

- [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/soundverse-ai-song-generator/id6479240046)
- [Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.soundverse.android)

If you subscribed inside the app, manage or cancel that subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings, not on the website.

## Soundverse for Chrome

Soundverse for Chrome (in preview) lets Agent One work in your own browser. It can fill in release forms, pitch playlist curators, register songs and research contacts while you watch and stay in control.

1. Add it from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/soundverse/gcdgdbohfjfkgjollhiopnjmljgpdfio). It's free.
2. While signed in to Soundverse, click the extension in Chrome's toolbar, then link it to your account. You can also go to [soundverse.ai/connect-browser](https://www.soundverse.ai/connect-browser).
3. Ask Agent One for something that happens on the web, and it opens the tab and works in your browser.

You can also click the toolbar button to send the current page to Agent One, or right-click a page, link, audio or video and choose **Send to Soundverse**.

The link between Soundverse and the extension is kept in your browser; nothing about it is sent to a server.
