## Yearly billing

Yearly plans cost less: Creator is $119.88 a year, Pro $299.88 and Max x1 $720, which saves up to 20%. On a yearly plan you still get your token allowance every month.

## Prices in India

In India, plans are charged in rupees: Creator ₹499 a month, Pro ₹1,299 and Max x1 ₹2,999.

## Student discount

Verified students aged 13 and up get 30% off Creator, Pro and Max. See [Student discount](/account/student-discount).

## Top-ups

On a paid plan, you can buy extra tokens at any time:

1. Click your name at the bottom of the left sidebar and choose **Manage your plan**.
2. Under **Upgrades & top-ups**, open the **Tokens** tab.
3. Pick a pack (10,000, 25,000, 50,000 or 100,000 tokens) and click **Buy once**, or enter a custom amount between 1,000 and 10,000,000 tokens.

Top-ups cost $1 for every 400 tokens (₹1 for every 4 tokens in India) and never expire. Your plan tokens are used first.

## Changing or cancelling your plan

Open **Manage your plan** to upgrade, downgrade or click **Cancel plan**. Upgrades are prorated and charged right away; downgrades are prorated with no immediate charge. When you cancel, your plan stays active until the end of the billing period, then your account moves to Free. See [Billing and payment](/faqs/billing-and-payment) for more.
