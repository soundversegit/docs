## Formats

| Plan | Audio formats |
| --- | --- |
| Free | MP3 |
| Creator and above | MP3, WAV and stems |

In AI Studio, **Export** offers more formats and video; see [AI Studio](/create/ai-studio#export).

## Monthly download limits

Each plan includes a number of audio downloads per calendar month:

| Plan | Downloads a month |
| --- | --- |
| Free | 5 |
| Creator | 50 |
| Pro | 150 |
| Max | 500 per Max level (for example, 1,000 on Max x2) |
| Enterprise | Unlimited |

Downloading the same file again doesn't count again. Playing music, and downloading cover art and videos, don't count either. When you reach your limit, you can upgrade to keep downloading.

## Verification check

If you download a lot of files in a short time (more than 20 in 10 minutes), we ask you to complete a quick check to confirm you're a person. Then your downloads continue.
