---
title: 'Managing Your Account'
---
<Accordion title="Which plan is right for me?">
  Go to https://www.soundverse.ai/pricing to compare the plans: Free, Creator, Pro, Max (x1 to x5, for more tokens) and Enterprise. Each has monthly and yearly pricing, and yearly billing saves up to 20%.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How do I see which Tier I'm in?">
  Click your name at the bottom of the left sidebar and choose **Manage your plan**. It shows your current plan and how many tokens you have left.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How do I Upgrade or Cancel my plan?">
  Click your name at the bottom of the left sidebar and choose **Manage your plan**. From there you can compare plans and upgrade, or click **Cancel plan**.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How do I make my profile private?">
  Click your name at the bottom of the left sidebar, then **My Account** → **Privacy Settings**, and turn on **Private profile**. Other people then see only your picture and @username, and your profile is kept out of Google. Songs you have published stay public; use **Make Private** in a song's ⋯ menu to hide one. It's free on every plan. See [Make Your Profile Private](/account/private-profile).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How do I delete my account?">
  Click your name at the bottom of the left sidebar, then **My Account** → **Privacy Settings**, and use **Delete account** in the Danger Zone. Your account is scheduled to close in 7 days and you're signed out. Sign in again before then and the deletion is paused, with nothing lost.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How do I change the app's language or theme?">
  Go to **My Account** → **Languages** to change the language, and **My Account** → **Appearance** to change the theme.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How do I choose which emails I get?">
  Go to **My Account** → **Notifications** and turn each kind of email or alert on or off.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="I deleted something by mistake. Can I get it back?">
  Yes, if it was recent. Go to **My Account** → **Recycle Bin**, find the track, video or image, and restore it.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How much storage do I have?">
  Go to **My Account** → **Storage** to see how much space you use and to find and remove large files.
</Accordion>
