title: 'Billing and payment'
---
<Accordion title="What is the pricing policy of Soundverse?">
  There are multiple tiers including a free plan. You can choose between Creator, Pro or Enterprise Tiers based on your requirement and usecase. To know more about the pricing policy, visit https://www.soundverse.ai/pricing.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="If I start a monthly subscription, is it just for one month or will it renew automatically?">
 - Your subscription renews automatically every month until you cancel it.
 - Your plan's tokens reset every month. When your plan renews, your plan token balance is set back to your plan's monthly allowance.
 - Unused plan tokens don't roll over. Any plan tokens left at the end of the month expire. For e.g. if your plan includes 10,000 tokens a month and you have 1,200 left when it renews, you start the new month with 10,000 tokens, not 11,200.
 - Tokens you buy as a top-up are kept separately from your plan tokens and don't expire. Your plan tokens are used first, then your top-up tokens.
 - For AppSumo users, your tokens will refresh to 1000 or 2600 tokens depending on Tier.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How do I buy additional tokens?">
   Simply navigate to "Manage Your Plan" under Profile Settings. Click on "Top Up Tokens" and slide to the number of tokens you'd want and click on "Buy Now". https://www.soundverse.ai/myaccount?index=2
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Why didn’t my credits refresh on my billing day?">
  It could be a technical error, so in that case, reach out at support@soundverse.ai for further help. You can even fill the feedback form for us to get back to you.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How do I cancel my plan?">
 Simply navigate to "Manage Your Plan" under Profile Settings. Press Cancel and you'll taken to cancellation process.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="What do I do if I accidentally selected a yearly plan instead of monthly?">
  You can always cancel the subscription at any point you want by following the steps mentioned. We'd also be happy to help you with the refund process.
  Make sure to reach out to us within 48 hours of this incident. 
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="If I upgrade from a lower plan to a higher plan, do I have to pay the full rate of the higher plan?">
  No, you just have to pay the remaining amount i.e. the difference amount of existing plan and the plan you are upgrading to.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Do you offer student or educator discounts?">
  We do not offer any student or educator discount as of now. We'd love to do it in the future.
  
  If you are an enterprise we can customize the plan for you. However, if you would like your school or institute to use our platform, then write to us to discuss further and get a deal.
</Accordion>
