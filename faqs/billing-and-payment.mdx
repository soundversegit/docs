<Accordion title="What is the pricing policy of Soundverse?">
  Soundverse has a Free plan and four paid plan families:

  | Plan | Monthly price (USD) | Tokens a month |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Free | $0 | 1,000 one-time trial tokens |
  | Creator | $12.49 | 4,000 |
  | Pro | $31.24 | 10,000 |
  | Max x1 to x5 | $72 to $290 | 25,000 to 125,000 |
  | Enterprise | Contact sales@soundverse.ai | Custom |

  Yearly billing costs less (Creator $119.88 a year, Pro $299.88, Max x1 $720). Prices in India are charged in rupees (Creator ₹499 a month, Pro ₹1,299, Max x1 ₹2,999). Higher plans also unlock newer Soundverse models, WAV and stem downloads, and wider licenses. See https://www.soundverse.ai/pricing for the full comparison.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="If I start a monthly subscription, is it just for one month or will it renew automatically?">
 - Your subscription renews automatically every month until you cancel it.
 - Your plan's tokens reset every month. When your plan renews, your plan token balance is set back to your plan's monthly allowance.
 - Unused plan tokens don't roll over. Any plan tokens left at the end of the month expire. For e.g. if your plan includes 10,000 tokens a month and you have 1,200 left when it renews, you start the new month with 10,000 tokens, not 11,200.
 - Tokens you buy as a top-up are kept separately from your plan tokens and don't expire. Your plan tokens are used first, then your top-up tokens.
 - On a yearly plan you still receive your monthly allowance every month.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How do I buy additional tokens?">
   Top-ups are available on paid plans.
   1. Click your name at the bottom of the left sidebar and choose **Manage your plan** (or go to https://www.soundverse.ai/account?tab=plan).
   2. Under **Upgrades & top-ups**, open the **Tokens** tab.
   3. Pick a pack (10,000, 25,000, 50,000 or 100,000 tokens) and click **Buy once**, or enter a custom amount between 1,000 and 10,000,000 tokens.

   Top-ups cost $1 for every 400 tokens (₹1 for every 4 tokens in India) and never expire.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Why didn’t my credits refresh on my billing day?">
  It could be a technical error, so in that case, reach out at support@soundverse.ai for further help. You can also use **Send feedback** in the menu under your name at the bottom of the left sidebar.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How do I cancel my plan?">
 Click your name at the bottom of the left sidebar, choose **Manage your plan**, and click **Cancel plan**. Your plan stays active until the end of your current billing period, then your account moves to Free and you won't be charged again.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="What do I do if I accidentally selected a yearly plan instead of monthly?">
  You can always cancel the subscription at any point you want by following the steps mentioned. We'd also be happy to help you with the refund process.
  Make sure to reach out to us within 48 hours of this incident. 
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="If I upgrade from a lower plan to a higher plan, do I have to pay the full rate of the higher plan?">
  No. When you upgrade, you're charged a prorated amount right away for the rest of the current period, so you only pay the difference. When you downgrade, the change is prorated without an immediate charge.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Do you offer student or educator discounts?">
  Yes. Verified students aged 13 and up get 30% off Creator, Pro and Max.
  1. Go to https://www.soundverse.ai/education and sign in.
  2. Enter your school email address. We email you a 6-digit code.
  3. Enter the code. The discount is applied automatically at checkout.

  A verification lasts a year, after which you confirm your school email again.

  If you would like your school or institution to use Soundverse, write to sales@soundverse.ai to discuss a plan.
</Accordion>
