<Accordion title="What can I do with Soundverse?">
 Soundverse is an AI music studio. Ask Agent One, our AI music producer, for a song, an instrumental, a video or a voiceover in plain words, and edit the results in AI Studio, a multitrack editor. You can also use any of the AI Tools on their own: song and music generation, stem separation, extend, remix, lyrics, voice cloning, Artist DNA and more.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Is Soundverse easy to use for beginners?">
  Soundverse is designed to be user-friendly, and beginners can get started with it relatively easily. However, there are advanced features for experienced users, making it suitable for musicians and content creators of all skill levels.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Is there a free trial available?">
 Yes. Every account starts on the Free plan with 1,000 one-time trial tokens, enough for about 3 full songs with Soundverse v6. Free songs download as MP3 and come with a Personal (non-commercial) license. See [pricing](https://www.soundverse.ai/pricing) for the paid plans.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How long can a track be?">
 You can make a track longer after it is generated: use **Extend Music** in AI Tools, or open the song's menu in your Library and choose **Edit**, then **Extend**. Each extension continues the track from where it ends. Extending an instrumental works on every plan; extending a song with Soundverse v7 or v8 needs Creator or above, and Soundverse v9 songs can't be extended yet.
</Accordion>

 Soundverse's commitment to innovation, versatility, and its strong focus on shaping the music industry through AI technology sets it apart from other platforms. Its wide range of functionalities and user-friendly interface make it a top choice for creators in the music industry.

<Accordion title="How do I generate music in Soundverse? ">
 Describe the song you want to Agent One in the chat, or open **AI Song Generator** (with vocals) or **AI Music Generator** (instrumental) from AI Tools and type a prompt. You can add your own lyrics, pick a style and choose the Soundverse model version your plan includes.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I customize the audio generated by Soundverse?">
  Yes. Open any song in AI Studio to arrange it on a multitrack timeline, split it into stems, add instruments and effects, or regenerate a section. You can also ask Agent One to make changes for you.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="What is a token?">
  A token is the unit of credit you spend on Soundverse. Each generation costs a set number of tokens, and the price depends on the tool and the model version, not on how long the result is. Every tool shows its price before you run it.
</Accordion>
