## What is Soundverse?

Soundverse is an AI music studio. Make songs, instrumentals, vocals, video and voiceovers by describing what you want, then edit, mix and release them, all in your browser.

There are four main ways to work:

- **Agent One** is our AI music producer. Tell it your goal in plain words, and it plans the steps and runs the right tools: lyrics, song, cover art, stems, music video and more.
- **AI Studio** is a multitrack studio with built-in instruments, effects, a mixer and AI generation on the timeline, plus real-time collaboration.
- **AI Tools** gives you each tool on its own, from AI Song Generator and Stem Separator to voice cloning and video tools.
- **Artist DNA** lets you train a model on your own music or voice, make new tracks in that style, and earn when others use it.

**Home** has a box to start a request, what's new, and today's **Sparks** (song ideas for what's happening now). Browse **Explore** and **Playlists** for music from the community, and keep everything you make in your **Library**.

Soundverse is free to start: every account gets 1,000 trial tokens. See [pricing](https://www.soundverse.ai/pricing) for the paid plans.
