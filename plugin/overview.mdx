Soundverse AI VST puts Soundverse inside the DAW you already use. The installer adds three plug-ins:

| Plug-in | What it does | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Soundverse AI VST** | Agent One, your Library, the AI tools, Sounds, DNA and your plugins, in tabs. Drag what you make straight onto your timeline. | Any track |
| **Soundverse AI FX** | Runs one of the effect plugins you build with Agent One. | An audio track |
| **Soundverse AI Instrument** | Plays one of the instrument plugins you build with Agent One from MIDI. | A MIDI or instrument track |

It's free to download and use with a free Soundverse account. AI tools you run inside it use your plan's tokens, at the same prices as on the web.

## System requirements

| | Windows | macOS | Linux |
|---|---|---|---|
| System | Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit (x64) | macOS 12 Monterey or later, Apple silicon or Intel | Ubuntu 24.04 or later, Debian 13, or another x86_64 distribution with glibc 2.38+ (.deb) |
| Memory | 4 GB RAM, 8 GB recommended | 4 GB RAM, 8 GB recommended | 4 GB RAM, 8 GB recommended |
| Disk space | About 150 MB | About 150 MB | About 100 MB |
| Plug-in formats | VST3, CLAP | VST3, Audio Unit, CLAP | VST3, CLAP |

You also need an internet connection to sign in, generate and sync. Plugins that have already synced keep playing offline.

<Note>
Pro Tools isn't supported yet: it only loads AAX plug-ins.
</Note>

## Sign in

Put **Soundverse AI VST** on any track and click **Sign in**. Your browser opens soundverse.ai with a code; approve it, and the plug-in signs in. One sign-in covers all three plug-ins, and it stays signed in after you restart your DAW. A project still plays if you sign out.
