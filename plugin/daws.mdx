---
title: 'Use it in your DAW'
description: 'Where to find Soundverse AI VST in Ableton Live, Logic Pro, GarageBand, REAPER, FL Studio, Cubase, Bitwig Studio and Pro Tools.'
---

After [installing](/plugin/install), open your DAW. If it was already open, restart it so it scans the new plug-ins. Look for **Soundverse**, **Soundverse FX** and **Soundverse Instrument** (manufacturer: Soundverse).

## Ableton Live

1. Open **Settings › Plug-Ins** (Windows: **Options › Preferences › Plug-Ins**).
2. Turn on **Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders**. On a Mac, **Use Audio Units v2** is optional.
3. Click **Rescan**. Hold **Option** (Mac) or **Alt** (Windows) while clicking it for a full rescan.
4. In the browser, open **Plug-Ins › VST3 › Soundverse** and drag **Soundverse** onto any track, **Soundverse FX** onto an audio track, or **Soundverse Instrument** onto a MIDI track.

## Logic Pro (Mac)

Logic loads Audio Units. On an audio track, click an empty **Audio FX** slot › **Audio Units › Soundverse › Soundverse**. Logic checks new Audio Units the first time it starts; if Soundverse is missing, open **Logic Pro › Settings › Plug-in Manager**, select it and click **Reset & Rescan Selection**.

<Note>
Soundverse FX and Soundverse Instrument are VST3 only, so they don't appear in Logic Pro or GarageBand yet. The main Soundverse plug-in does.
</Note>

## GarageBand (Mac)

Select a track, open **Smart Controls** (press **B**), then **Plug-ins** and click an empty slot › **Audio Units › Soundverse › Soundverse**.

## REAPER

Click a track's **FX** button, then look under **VST3** for **Soundverse**. If it's missing, open **Options › Preferences › Plug-ins › VST** and click **Re-scan**. REAPER also loads the CLAP version.

## FL Studio

1. Open **Options › Manage plugins** and click **Start scan** (**Find installed plugins** in some versions). FL Studio scans the standard VST3 folder.
2. For **Soundverse** or **Soundverse FX**, click an empty slot in a **Mixer** insert and pick it.
3. For **Soundverse Instrument**, click **+** in the **Channel rack** and pick it.

## Cubase

Cubase scans new plug-ins when it starts. Click an empty **Inserts** slot on an audio track and pick **Soundverse** or **Soundverse FX**. For **Soundverse Instrument**, add an **Instrument Track** and choose it as the instrument. If something is missing, check **Studio › VST Plug-in Manager**.

## Bitwig Studio

Open the browser and search **Soundverse** under **Devices** (VST3 or CLAP). If it's missing, open **Settings › Locations › Plug-ins**, make sure the system locations are on, and rescan.

## Pro Tools

Not supported yet. Pro Tools only loads AAX plug-ins, and Soundverse AI VST is VST3, Audio Unit and CLAP.

## Other DAWs

Any 64-bit DAW that loads VST3 (Windows, Mac, Linux), Audio Units (Mac) or CLAP plug-ins works, such as Studio One. Rescan plug-ins in its plug-in manager, then look for Soundverse.

## Load your plugins on a track

1. Put **Soundverse FX** on an audio track or **Soundverse Instrument** on a MIDI track.
2. Open **Soundverse** on any track and go to the **Plugins** tab. The tracks with an empty Soundverse slot appear under **In your DAW**.
3. Hover a plugin and click **Load**, then pick the track. It syncs from your account the first time, so a plugin you built a minute ago is there too.

Use **All**, **Instrument** and **Effect** at the top of Your Plugins to filter the list.
