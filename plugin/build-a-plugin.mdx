---
title: 'Build your own plugin'
description: 'Make effects and instruments with Agent One, play them in AI Studio and load them in your DAW.'
---

Agent One builds effect and instrument plugins from a description, each with its own faceplate. You can play them in AI Studio and load them in your DAW with [Soundverse AI VST](/plugin/overview).

## Build one

1. Ask Agent One, for example *"a warm tape saturation effect"* or *"a soft pad synth with a slow attack"*. You can also open the [AI Plugin Generator](https://www.soundverse.ai/features/ai-plugin-generator) from AI Tools.
2. Agent One builds the plugin and shows it in the chat with its faceplate. Try it on the chat's song, or attach a file from your Library to hear it on your own audio.
3. To change it, just ask: *"make the knobs bigger"*, *"add a wet/dry mix"* or *"restyle it like a vintage tape machine"*. Each change saves a new version.

## Cost

| | Tokens |
|---|---|
| Build a plugin (effect or instrument) | 100 |
| Change or restyle it | Free |

If a build fails, the 100 tokens are refunded. It works on every plan, Free included.

## Use it in AI Studio

Your plugins appear in the Studio's effects and instruments list under **My plugins**. Click the star next to any effect, instrument or plugin to pin it to the **★ Starred** group at the top.

## Use it in your DAW

Install [Soundverse AI VST](/plugin/install), sign in, and your plugins are in the **Plugins** tab. [Load one on a track](/plugin/daws#load-your-plugins-on-a-track) through Soundverse FX or Soundverse Instrument. They process audio on your computer and keep playing offline once synced.
