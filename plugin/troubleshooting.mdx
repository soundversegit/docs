## It doesn't show up in my DAW

- Restart your DAW, then rescan plug-ins ([how, per DAW](/plugin/daws)).
- Check that VST3 (or Audio Units on a Mac) is turned on in your DAW's plug-in settings.
- Make sure your DAW is 64-bit and loads VST3, Audio Unit or CLAP. Pro Tools (AAX only) isn't supported yet.
- On a Mac, check the files are there: in Finder press **Shift+Cmd+G** and go to `/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3`.

## "This Audio Unit plug-in could not be opened", or the VST3s are missing on a Mac

Beta 6 and earlier needed **macOS 15** and had a signature problem that made some DAWs skip the VST3s. Download the latest installer from [soundverse.ai/features/vst-plugin](https://www.soundverse.ai/features/vst-plugin) and install it over the old one, then rescan in your DAW.

## macOS or Windows blocks the installer

The beta installers aren't code-signed yet. On a Mac use **System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway**; on Windows click **More info › Run anyway**. Full steps: [Install](/plugin/install).

## Previews take a while to start

The first time you play a song, the plug-in downloads it, so a long song can take a few seconds on a slow connection. The play button shows a spinner while it loads. After that it plays straight away.

## Still stuck?

Email [help@soundverse.ai](mailto:help@soundverse.ai) with your computer (Mac or Windows and its version), your DAW and its version, and a screenshot.
