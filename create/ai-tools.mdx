Open **AI Tools** in the left sidebar to run any tool on its own, or ask [Agent One](/create/agent) and it picks the tools for you. Many tools also work inside [AI Studio](/create/ai-studio) from a track or clip's right-click menu.

Each tool's page on soundverse.ai shows what it costs in tokens. Tools marked Creator or Pro need that plan or above.

## Create music and vocals

| Tool | What it does | Plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [AI Song Generator](https://www.soundverse.ai/features/ai-song-generator) | Describe a song or paste your lyrics, and get a full song with vocals, a title and cover art. | Every plan |
| [AI Music Generator](https://www.soundverse.ai/features/ai-music-generator) | Describe the mood, genre and instruments, and get an original instrumental track with a title and cover art. | Every plan |
| [AI Singing Generator](https://www.soundverse.ai/features/ai-singing-generator) | Type your lyrics, pick a vocal style, and get a sung a cappella vocal with no backing track. | Every plan |
| [AI Lyrics Writer](https://www.soundverse.ai/features/ai-lyrics-writer) | Tell it what the song is about, pick a mood, section and genre, and get original lyrics to edit and sing. | Every plan |
| [AI Lyrics Co-Writer](https://www.soundverse.ai/features/ai-lyrics-co-writer) | Co-write a song with AI — set the brief, generate a draft, then refine it line by line. | Every plan |
| [Melody to Song Generator](https://www.soundverse.ai/features/melody-to-song-generator) | Start from your own melody and let AI build the complete song around it. | Every plan |
| [MIDI to Song Generator](https://www.soundverse.ai/features/midi-to-song-generator) | Turn a MIDI melody and your lyrics into a complete, sung song with a title and cover art. | Every plan |
| [Train Voice Clone](https://www.soundverse.ai/features/voice-clone) | Train an AI voice clone from a short sample of your singing, then sing new songs with it in the AI Song Generator. | Creator and above |
