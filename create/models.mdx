Song and music tools such as AI Song Generator and AI Music Generator have a **Model** menu. Agent One picks the newest Soundverse version your plan includes; to choose a model yourself, open a tool and pick one from its Model menu.

## Soundverse models

| Model | Plan | Good to know |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soundverse v9 | Pro and above | The newest version. Can't extend songs. 500 tokens a song. |
| Soundverse v8 | Creator and above | Where Creator accounts start. Can extend songs. 400 tokens a song. |
| Soundverse v7 | Creator and above | 400 tokens a song. |
| Soundverse v6 | Every plan | Where Free accounts start. 300 tokens a song. |

Only the Soundverse models can sing in your voice clone or follow a reference song. None of them has a length setting: the model decides how long each song runs, and you can make it longer with Extend Music (v6 to v8).

<Note>
On October 5, 2026, every Soundverse version moved up one number, and v9 was added. The model you knew as v5 is now v6, v6 is now v7, and v7 is now v8.
</Note>

## Other music models

| Model | Plan | Good to know |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ElevenMusic v2.5 | Creator and above | Sings your lyrics exactly as written, section by section. Set a length from 15 seconds to 5 minutes, follow a reference clip, make loops or score a video. Also in Inpainting. Priced by length. |
| Google Lyria 3.5 | Creator and above | Text only: a description and optional lyrics in, one complete song or instrumental out. No reference, length or voice clone. |

## Video models

Video tools offer Seedance 2.0 (the default), Seedance 2.5, Veo 3.1 and Gemini Omni Flash 1.1, on the Creator plan and above.
