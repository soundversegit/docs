DJ One is a two-deck DJ booth that runs in your browser and plays the songs in your Soundverse Library. It has scratchable vinyl platters, SYNC, hot cues, loops, keylock, a 3-band EQ and crossfader, performance effects, Automix and Agent One in the booth.

DJ One is included on every plan, and mixing doesn't use tokens. Asking Agent One in the booth to make a track, and analysing a song's sections, do use tokens.

## Start mixing

1. Click **Create** in the left sidebar and choose **DJ**.
2. Drag songs from your Library onto **Deck A** and **Deck B**, or drop an MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A or OGG file from your computer onto a deck. Dropped files are added to your Library.
3. Press play, use **SYNC** to match tempos and move the crossfader to blend.

Click the question-mark button for a guided first mix, or press **?** for every keyboard shortcut.

## Automix

Turn on **AUTOMIX** and DJ One mixes for you: as a song nears its end, it picks a transition that suits the two songs' key, tempo and energy, syncs the next deck and crossfades. Grab the crossfader at any time to take over.

## DJ controllers

In a browser that supports Web MIDI, DJ One works with a connected MIDI controller using a default layout (channel 1 for Deck A, channel 2 for Deck B). Controllers with a different layout may not line up, and controls can't be remapped yet.

## Good to know

DJ One plays your Soundverse Library and files you add. It doesn't stream from other music services, and you need the rights to anything you upload.
