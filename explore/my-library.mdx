Click **Library** in the left sidebar. Your Library holds everything you've made, uploaded or saved, sorted into tabs:

| Tab | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| Projects | Your chats and AI Studio projects |
| Audios | Songs, instrumentals, stems, recordings and MIDI files |
| Videos | Videos you've generated or uploaded |
| Images | Cover art and other images |
| Prompts | Prompts you've used |
| Playlists | Playlists you've created, saved, or that were shared with you |
| Documents | Documents you've attached to chats |

Each tab has filters across the top. For example, **Audios** can show only Generated, Uploaded, MIDI, Recorded, Downloaded or AI Studio files. **Saved** shows public tracks by other people that you saved from Explore; they stay theirs and aren't mixed into **All**.

## Working with a track

Open a track's menu to play, download, rename, open it in AI Studio, edit it (for example **Extend**), or choose **Make Private** or **Publish**. To buy a license for a track, open its page and click **License** (see [Licensing](/faqs/licensing-model)).

## Uploading

Drag files into your Library or use the upload button. You need the rights to anything you upload.

## Downloads

Free plan downloads are MP3. Creator and above can also download WAV and stems. Each plan includes a monthly number of downloads; see [pricing](https://www.soundverse.ai/pricing).

## Deleted files and storage

Deleted tracks, videos and images go to the **Recycle Bin** (**My Account** → **Recycle Bin**), where you can restore them or delete them forever. To see how much space you use and remove large files, go to **My Account** → **Storage**.
